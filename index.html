About the firm

A strategic advisory and business growth platform.

Zeka BIR drives scalable growth, operational transformation and strategic execution across the FinTech, NBFC, MSME lending and technology ecosystems.

The firm works with startups, financial institutions and growth-stage businesses to strengthen market positioning, expand operations, build scalable business models and accelerate sustainable growth. Engagements cover business transformation, operating models, growth strategy, partnerships, stakeholder management, sales enablement and operational scalability.

Alongside advisory, Zeka BIR invests in early-stage companies and takes an active role in business building, strategic execution and market expansion for its portfolio.

Its focus is long-term value creation through institutional growth, scalable lending ecosystems and technology-led business innovation.

Approach

How an engagement runs.

Four stages, in order. Most of the work sits in the last two.

STAGE 01

Assess

A close read of economics, portfolio, channels, organisation and systems, to separate a growth problem from a structural one and put numbers against both.

STAGE 02

Plan

The operating model, product construct and capital plan that follow from the assessment, agreed with the promoter and sequenced against available cash.

STAGE 03

Execute

Working alongside your team to open partnerships, launch lending programmes, install the sales motion and set a reporting cadence that holds.

STAGE 04

Institutionalise

Handover into governance: leadership capability, MIS, controls and an investor-grade narrative, so the business runs and raises without external support.
